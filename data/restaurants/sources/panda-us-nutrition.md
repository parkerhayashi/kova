# Nutrition & Allergen information

Panda uses ingredients that contain all the major FDA allergens (peanuts, tree nuts, eggs, fish, shellfish, milk, soy, wheat and sesame). Panda prepares its entrees fresh with shared cooking equipment and therefore allergens could be present in any entree. No MSG added. Some ingredients may contain naturally occurring MSG.

Panda Express is committed to providing accurate nutritional and ingredient information, however, because our food is handcrafted and made in small batches, there may be differences between the actual nutritional content of your order and the nutritional values provided. Nutritional values are based on standard recipes and serving methods, but actual nutritional content may vary due to factors including, without limitation, ingredient and supplier differences, portion size and preparation variations, cooking methods and equipment, regional or seasonal differences, and natural variability in raw materials. Certain menu items, limited‑time offers, regional or test items may not be available at all locations or reflected in the information provided.
